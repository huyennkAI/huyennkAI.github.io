02 — Research

Domains I Explore

⚡

Optimization Dynamics & Grokking

Studying the geometry of SGD trajectories in deep networks — when and why over-parameterized models suddenly generalize after appearing stuck, and how spectral methods can shape the optimizer trajectory deliberately.

∞

Implicit-Depth & Fixed-Point Models

Exploring deep equilibrium architectures with weight-tied layers, Anderson-accelerated fixed-point iteration, and orthogonalized optimizers (Muon, Newton–Schulz) — elegant, memory-efficient, and mathematically rich.

🛡️

Safety-Preserving LLM Fine-Tuning

How can low-rank parameter-efficient adaptation (LoRA, QLoRA) preserve instruction-format alignment under domain shift? Practical and theoretical sides of this question — especially for low-resource languages like Vietnamese.

🎯

Reinforcement Learning

Off-policy RL with Bellman-target methods, Q-learning for sequential decision-making, and the broader question of how RL agents organize their value functions during training.

🧮

Mathematical Foundations

Convex/nonconvex optimization, spectral methods (SVD, Newton–Schulz), stochastic processes, and the analytical machinery — Riemannian/information geometry, RKHS — that underpins modern ML theory.
