I'm Nguyen Khanh Huyen, graduating early in 2026 from Foreign Trade University with a focus on the mathematical foundations of modern machine learning.

My research centers on three intertwined questions: the optimization dynamics of deep learning (grokking, SGD trajectories, implicit-depth models), safety-preserving parameter-efficient adaptation of foundation models, and the statistical/geometric foundations of generalization.

As a TPer'61 and FTUer'61, I came to ML from a background in mathematical olympiads and quantitative economics — and I'm now transitioning toward doctoral research at the intersection of optimization theory and large-scale language model training.
